    Subtyping Rules of Capless #

    Subbound Γ B1 B2 corresponds to Bound Subtyping in Fig. 2. The mutually-recursive inductive definitions ESubtyp, CSubtyp, and SSubtyp correspond to the subtyping rules in Fig. 2.
