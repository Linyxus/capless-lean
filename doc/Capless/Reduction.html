    Operational Semantics of System Capless #

    The reduction relation works on evaluation states, which is a triplet of a store, a continuation stack and a redex. See Store.

    Most rules of Reduce correspond directly to the paper definitions, and adapted for the triplet representation of the evaluation state.

    source
    inductive Capless.Reduce {n m k n' m' k' : ℕ} :
    State n m k → State n' m' k' → Prop

    The reduction relation defined in Fig. 10 and 6.

    • apply {n m k : ℕ} {x : Fin n} {T : CType n m k} {t : Term (n + 1) m k} {cont : Cont n m k} {y : Fin n} {σ : Store n m k} : σ.Bound x (λ(x:T)t) → ⟨σ | cont | Term.app x y⟩ ---> ⟨σ | cont | t.open y⟩
    • tapply {n m k : ℕ} {x : Fin n} {S : SType n m k} {t : Term n (m + 1) k} {cont : Cont n m k} {X : Fin m} {σ : Store n m k} : σ.Bound x (λ[X<:S]t) → ⟨σ | cont | Term.tapp x X⟩ ---> ⟨σ | cont | t.topen X⟩
    • capply {n m k : ℕ} {x : Fin n} {B : CBound n k} {t : Term n m (k + 1)} {cont : Cont n m k} {c : Fin k} {σ : Store n m k} : σ.Bound x (λ[c<:B]t) → ⟨σ | cont | Term.capp x c⟩ ---> ⟨σ | cont | t.copen c⟩
    • enter {n m k : ℕ} {σ : Store n m k} {cont : Cont n m k} {S : SType n m k} {t : Term (n + 1) m (k + 1)} : ⟨σ | cont | boundary:S in t⟩ ---> ⟨(σ.label S).cval {x=0} | Cont.scope 0 cont.weaken.cweaken | t⟩
    • leave_var {n m k : ℕ} {σ : Store n m k} {y : Fin n} {cont : Cont n m k} {x : Fin n} : ⟨σ | Cont.scope x cont | Term.var y⟩ ---> ⟨σ | cont | Term.var y⟩
    • leave_val {n m k : ℕ} {σ : Store n m k} {cont : Cont n m k} {x : Fin n} {v : Term n m k} (hv : v.IsValue) : ⟨σ | Cont.scope x cont | v⟩ ---> ⟨σ | cont | v⟩
    • invoke {n m k : ℕ} {x : Fin n} {S : SType n m k} {tail : Cont n m k} {y : Fin n} {σ : Store n m k} {cont : Cont n m k} : σ.LBound x S → cont.HasLabel x tail → ⟨σ | cont | Term.invoke x y⟩ ---> ⟨σ | tail | Term.var y⟩
    • push {n m k : ℕ} {σ : Store n m k} {cont : Cont n m k} {t : Term n m k} {u : Term (n + 1) m k} : ⟨σ | cont | let x=t in u⟩ ---> ⟨σ | Cont.cons u cont | t⟩
    • push_ex {n m k : ℕ} {σ : Store n m k} {cont : Cont n m k} {t : Term n m k} {u : Term (n + 1) m (k + 1)} : ⟨σ | cont | let(c,x)=t in u⟩ ---> ⟨σ | Cont.conse u cont | t⟩
    • rename {n m k : ℕ} {σ : Store n m k} {u : Term (n + 1) m k} {cont : Cont n m k} {x : Fin n} : ⟨σ | Cont.cons u cont | Term.var x⟩ ---> ⟨σ | cont | u.open x⟩
    • lift {n m k : ℕ} {v : Term n m k} {σ : Store n m k} {u : Term (n + 1) m k} {cont : Cont n m k} (hv : v.IsValue) : ⟨σ | Cont.cons u cont | v⟩ ---> ⟨σ.val v hv | cont.weaken | u⟩
    • lift_ex {n m k : ℕ} {σ : Store n m k} {u : Term (n + 1) m (k + 1)} {cont : Cont n m k} {C : CaptureSet n k} {x : Fin n} : ⟨σ | Cont.conse u cont | Term.pack C x⟩ ---> ⟨σ.cval C | cont.cweaken | u.open x⟩
